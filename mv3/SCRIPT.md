# Accretion — directing script (mv3)

**Logline.** A world is built grain by grain out of a weather forecast, paid for by viewers like you,
and then given back.

**Only hard reference:** the song (Frums — Credits EX). The voice is a weather forecast and the end-of-show
sponsor credits of a public broadcast; that theme lives in how matter behaves, never as on-screen text.

## Ground rules

1. No interface. No panels, labels, prompts or readable words until the last line of the film.
2. One continuous take. No cuts: every section is the previous one changing state.
3. Everything is simulated. Music adds energy: kicks push, loudness feeds, each spoken syllable adds matter.
4. The voice is matter, not words: a syllable is a grain, a branch, a ripple, a body.
5. Restraint: true black, one material at a time, 2-3 colours each, most of the screen empty.
6. Beats below are the song's step grid (179 BPM, 1/8-beat steps, shared with the original project).

## Shot list

### 1 · Seed — 0:00–0:05 (intro riser, to beat 60)
Black. One `·` at the centre. As the riser climbs, faint single dots flicker in a circle around it whose radius
follows loudness. In the last half second the circle collapses inward. **Beat 60, the drop:** the seed bursts
into a few hundred grains thrown upward; they become the first rain.

### 2 · Weather → sand — 0:05–0:48 (beats 60–1080, the forecast)
Braille-dot resolution (240×144). A cloud layer forms along the top and drifts west → east ("west winds").
Every syllable of the forecast rains a handful of grains from where the cloud is thickest; grains fall pale blue,
**turn to sand on landing**, and pile into dunes that slump into small avalanches on kicks.
- "Tonight: mostly cloudy, isolated showers" (beat 328–): cool palette, clouds thicken, rain in isolated columns.
- **0:27 hit (beat 570):** the ground breathes — the top layer of every dune is thrown up and falls again.
- "Friday: sunny, lush colour" (beat 659–): the clouds thin and part, a warm glow rises at the top right,
  the sand turns gold.
- "Saturday… High-igh-igh" (beat 1009–): the sun-glow flickers with each "igh"; from beat 1044 time stutters —
  the falling grains jerk back upward in growing hiccups, matching the voice breaking up. Beat 1079: still.

### 3 · Fire — 0:48–1:20 (beats 1080–1848, the drop)
**Beat 1080:** the dunes ignite left to right in one sweep. Flames rise from the dune surface in a heat field
(half-block pixels, fire palette); flame height follows the bass; snares throw single sparks (`·` `•`).
The dunes burn down slowly; what's left is a low line of ash.
- Beat 1336 (more energy): flames taller.
- **1:15 hit (beat 1722):** the fire inhales — every flame is pulled to the centre, held, then exhaled.
- Beats 1784–1848: the fire dies to embers glowing in the ash.

### 4 · Growth — 1:20–2:08 (beats 1848–2976, "Funding for this program…")
From the embers, stems grow up out of the ash, drawn with rounded line characters (`│ ─ ╭ ╮ ╰ ╯ ├ ┤`).
Each spoken word extends every growing tip by one cell. **Each "by" splits a branch; each "like you"
opens leaves** (braille dots) at the tips.
- "Broadcast Corporation" (beat 2354–): new growth turns rigid — long straight runs, right angles, heavy lines,
  steel colour, a lattice. Leaves stop.
- **1:53 hit (beat 2618):** every leaf falls at once.
- As the voice corrupts ("cor… po…", beat 2700–), the lattice cracks: segments break and fall as dust.
- "CONNECTION LOST" (beat 2900–): the cracks run through the whole structure.

### 5 · Erosion — 2:08–2:14 (beats 2976–3132, the break)
The structure dissolves into drifting dust that thins to nothing. One dot remains at the centre, pulsing like a
heartbeat (a double pulse every bar). **2:11 hit (beat 3066):** the outline of the whole lost structure flashes
once, faintly, then is gone.

### 6 · Crystal — 2:14–2:25 (beats 3132–3390, "Annual financial support")
The heartbeat dot becomes a seed crystal. Wandering particles drift in and stick on contact, stamped with
six-fold symmetry, so a snowflake grows. Each "Annual / financial / suppor-" syllable releases a burst of
walkers; growth speeds up with the build-up and races in the last half second (beats 3376–3390).

### 7 · Life — 2:25–2:46 (beats 3390–3896, the chaos)
**Beat 3390:** the crystal shatters into living cells: the field runs a cellular automaton, one generation per step.
Kicks drop new patterns into it; the chopped voice ("by ci po po cor cor…") sprinkles cells. **The rule set
changes every two bars**, so the texture and colour change on the bar line.
- **2:38 hit (beat 3706):** almost every cell dies at once; one glider survives and keeps going while the
  field slowly refills.
- "This is PBS!" (beat 3865–3877): the field freezes and fades; only a single glider is left moving.

### 8 · Water — 2:46–3:10 (beats 3896–4460, the Logan Airport observations)
The last glider falls into still, dark water: the first ripple. Each syllable of the observations is a drop;
rings spread and interfere (a wave simulation, drawn as fine braille rings). The whole surface drifts slowly
towards the north-east ("wind southwest at 13"); tiny droplets condense out of nowhere ("humidity 46 percent");
"pressure… rising" (beat 4319–) — the water brightens as if the level rises.
- **2:49 hit (beat 3963):** one huge drop shakes the whole surface; a single pale flash.
- Beats 4401–4411: the surface flickers and freezes; the brightest crests stay as points.

### 9 · Orbit — 3:10–3:53 (beats 4460–5500, "Searching…" / chopped "funding")
The frozen points begin to orbit a centre under gravity, leaving faint braille trails. Each chopped syllable
adds a new body. On kicks the nearest body sends a short ping ring outward (searching). Over the section the
bodies are slowly drawn into one circular ring.

### 10 · Light — 3:53–4:36 (beats 5500–6508, the final drop)
**Beat 5500:** the ring thickens into a rotating torus drawn with shading characters (lit, truecolor).
Its surface is banded with every earlier material: sand, fire, growth, crystal, life, water, orbit — the world
so far, as one object. It breathes with the bass; each syllable of "Funding for this program was made made
made…" knocks it a notch.
- **4:01 hit (beat 5690):** it freezes, pure white, for a beat.
- **4:15 hit (beat 6010, "…viewers like you"):** it shatters into grains that hang in the air.
- **4:23 hit (beat 6202):** for a moment the screen splits in four: the grain cloud drawn as sand, fire,
  crystal and water at once.
- Beat 6270 onward: the grains swirl into a slow vortex.

### 11 · Settle — 4:36–end (beat 6508 onward)
Gravity returns. Every grain falls and piles into one low dune. The last grain lands as the music ends.
Silence. A cursor blinks once, then types, small and centred:

    made possible by viewers like you.
